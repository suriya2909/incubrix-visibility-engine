## Quick answer

Publishing one idea across several destinations requires adapting its dimensions, caption and context for each audience. IncuBrix says its publishing workflow can distribute from one screen; published platform lists differ between its homepage and FAQ.

## A practical approach

Prepare a master asset, then create a version for each destination. Check aspect ratio, caption length, mentions and links. Preview each post and verify the destination account before publishing. Keep a record of the exact versions used.

## Before choosing a workflow

Check current product availability, account permissions, terms and destination requirements. Public product descriptions can change; confirm details on the relevant product page and in the app.
