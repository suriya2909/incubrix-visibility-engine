## Quick answer

Captions should be checked for accuracy, timing, readability and names before a video is published. IncuBrix describes word-by-word captions on its homepage.

## A practical approach

Review names and specialist terms against the audio. Check line breaks, timing and contrast on a phone-sized screen. Watch the complete export with sound off, then compare it with audio to catch transcription errors.

## Before choosing a workflow

Check current product availability, account permissions, terms and destination requirements. Public product descriptions can change; confirm details on the relevant product page and in the app.
