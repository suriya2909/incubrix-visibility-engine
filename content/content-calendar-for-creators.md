## A practical approach

Start with a realistic publishing cadence. Group topics into themes, connect each topic to a format, and leave room to adapt timely ideas. Track status from idea through review and publication. Avoid scheduling content before checking the current platform support and approval workflow.

## Before choosing a workflow

Check current product availability, account permissions, terms and destination requirements. Public product descriptions can change; confirm details on the relevant product page and in the app.
