## Quick answer

A small content team can reduce publishing errors by assigning clear review steps: draft, edit, factual check, approval and publish. IncuBrix describes shared workspace and approval concepts on its homepage, with some team features marked coming soon.

## A practical approach

Name one owner for each stage. Keep the source, final copy and destination together. Confirm rights, links and claims before approval. Use a checklist for content that includes collaborators or client work.

## Before choosing a workflow

Check current product availability, account permissions, terms and destination requirements. Public product descriptions can change; confirm details on the relevant product page and in the app.
