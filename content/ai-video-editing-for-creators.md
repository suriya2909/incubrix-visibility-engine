## Quick answer

Creators evaluating an AI video editor should check input formats, transcript editing, clip selection, captions, export controls and review steps. IncuBrix describes transcript-based editing, clip creation and captions on its homepage.

## A practical approach

Test the tool with a representative recording. Check whether edits map cleanly to the video, inspect captions and exports, and review licensing and privacy terms for any voice or avatar feature. Keep a human review step before publishing.

## Before choosing a workflow

Check current product availability, account permissions, terms and destination requirements. Public product descriptions can change; confirm details on the relevant product page and in the app.

## Next step

Compare the workflow against your actual recording and destination requirements.
