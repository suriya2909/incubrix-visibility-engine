## Five verified observations

1. The homepage says Creator Studio is live and describes content insights, creation and publishing. Source: https://incubrix.com/
2. The homepage labels Content Performance as rolling out and some capabilities, including translation and team workspace, as coming soon. Source: https://incubrix.com/
3. The How It Works page labels scheduling as soon to be available and its Track & Grow stage as coming soon. Source: https://incubrix.com/how-it-works
4. Platform lists differ: the homepage names YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Spotify and Bluesky, while the FAQ says YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn and Spotify, with TikTok on the roadmap. Source: https://incubrix.com/ and https://incubrix.com/faq
5. Pricing statements differ: FAQ lists Free, Starter $29, Creator $69, Pro $129 and Business $249 per month; the pricing page lists Free, Starter $10, Essential $20, Growth $50, Scale $100 and Pro $250 per month. Confirm current product prices before publishing. Sources: https://incubrix.com/faq and https://incubrix.com/pricing

## Three evidence-backed opportunities

1. Reconcile platform availability copy across homepage, FAQ and How It Works, then make destination support explicit.
2. Reconcile pricing names, amounts, credit limits and billing terms between FAQ and pricing page.
3. Clarify live versus forthcoming features (including analytics and scheduling) consistently and add an owner-reviewed last-review date to changing product details.

These are copy consistency opportunities, not measured search visibility gaps. No search ranking or AI citation finding has been observed.
