## Follow-up public-page review - 2026-10-25

Current accessible text review reconfirmed several high-priority content consistency issues. The homepage says publishing supports YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Spotify and Bluesky, while the FAQ lists YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn and says TikTok is on the roadmap. The homepage labels Content Performance as 'Rolling out now' in navigation but 'Coming soon' in the feature section; the homepage also presents analytics-related claims while How It Works marks Track & Grow as coming soon. Pricing remains inconsistent: FAQ states Starter $29, Creator $69, Pro $129 and Business $249 per month, while Pricing lists Starter $10, Essential $20, Growth $50, Scale $100 and Pro $250 per month. Pricing describes monthly credits but its FAQ says usage limits are available over a one-year period. How It Works displays '+164%' without an explanatory source or methodology in the accessible page text. These need product-owner confirmation before rewriting public claims.
