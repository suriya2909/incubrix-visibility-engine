# 90-day launch plan (13 weeks)

**All targets are proposed goals, not achieved results.** Numerical uplift targets should be set after Week 1 baselines and stakeholder approval. Organic signup targets require verified analytics attribution; otherwise report signups as unavailable. AI answer share is a sampled manual measure, not total market share.

| Week | Objective and work | Owner | Tools | Deliverable | KPI / measurement | Dependencies and risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline and access: Confirm owners, markets, analytics access and product claims. | Growth lead + product | Search Console, analytics, browser | Access checklist; baseline protocol | Capture current clicks, impressions, positions and conversions if permitted. | Analytics access and verified signup events; privacy limits. |
| 2 | Crawl and indexability: Run crawl; inspect robots, sitemap, canonicals and index reports. | Technical SEO | Crawler, Search Console | Crawl findings and index map | Index coverage and crawl errors from actual tools. | Site access; staging/prod differences. |
| 3 | Performance: Measure templates on mobile and desktop; prioritize field data. | Web engineer | PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse, CrUX | Performance backlog | Core Web Vitals field and lab metrics where available. | Traffic threshold for field data; third-party scripts. |
| 4 | Information architecture: Map topic hubs, navigation and internal links. | SEO + product marketing | Crawler, Search Console | Internal link map | Crawl depth and relevant internal links. | URL changes need redirects. |
| 5 | Claims and page alignment: Reconcile platform, feature and pricing copy across pages/app. | Product marketing + legal | Product app, pricing, FAQ | Approved claims inventory | Count of reconciled claims; no unsupported claim targets. | Product owner review. |
| 6 | Question research: Collect first candidate questions from actual creator research and SERPs. | Research analyst | Search, customer interviews, spreadsheets | Sourced question bank v1 | Questions with source, date, market and status. | No query-volume assumptions without data. |
| 7 | Measurement pilot: Run first day in US plus two chosen markets across Google and four assistants. | Research analyst | Browser sessions, evidence capture | Run 1 observations | Coverage by platform/market; completion rate. | Manual access, personalization, rate limits. |
| 8 | Answer-first page updates: Improve existing pages for clear direct answers and evidence. | Content + subject experts | CMS, editorial review | Approved page changes | Organic visibility baseline; crawl/index checks. | Do not publish unverified product claims. |
| 9 | Content batch 1: Publish only fact-checked research-led pages. | Content lead | CMS, sources, analytics | Approved content batch | Impressions/clicks and indexing after sufficient time. | Indexing and attribution lag. |
| 10 | Citation research: Review real search results and assistant citations for target queries. | Research analyst | Search/browser, evidence log | Evidence-backed citation prospects | Verified observed source count toward 30. | Results vary by locale and sampling. |
| 11 | Second measurement day: Repeat same query, platform, market and settings; retain evidence. | Research analyst | Same capture setup | Run 2 observations | Comparable-check coverage and descriptive changes. | Sampling variation; model changes. |
| 12 | Iteration and technical validation: Recheck implemented fixes and tune content from observed evidence. | SEO + engineering | Crawler, validators, Search Console | Validated fixes and update log | Fix status with actual test results. | Requires production deployment. |
| 13 | Review and next-quarter plan: Summarize baselines, limits and next experiments. | Growth lead | Dashboard, analytics, evidence files | Assessment report and next plan | Organic visibility and answer-share definitions finalized from baseline. | Organic signups only if analytics attribution is implemented and validated. |

## Proposed goal setting after baseline

- Organic visibility: establish clicks, impressions and query coverage; set an approved improvement goal after baseline and seasonality review.
- Answer share: define sampled answer share as verified brand mentions divided by completed comparable answers; set a goal after two consistent runs.
- AI mention rate: report by assistant, market and question cohort; do not generalize beyond the sample.
- Organic signups: set a target only after consent-compliant analytics events, attribution and conversion deduplication have been validated.
